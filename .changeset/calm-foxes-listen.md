Adds chat transcript components, adapted from shadcn/ui. `@shipfox/react-ui/message-scroller` follows a streaming reply, keeps a sent message near the top, and offers `Jump to latest`. `@shipfox/react-ui/message` aligns a turn and holds a footer for short status. `@shipfox/react-ui/bubble` is a chat bubble, with a `muted` variant for the agent. The scroller depends on `@shadcn/react`.
