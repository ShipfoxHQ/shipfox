Adds Jam to integrations: connect a Jam workspace with a personal access token, see which Jam user the connection acts as, and replace its token. Integrations connected with a pasted credential now link to their settings page from the integration menu. Replacing the credential of an already connected integration now works when the page has not loaded that connection yet.
