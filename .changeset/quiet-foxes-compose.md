Adds chat components, adapted from shadcn/ui. `@shipfox/react-ui/prompt-input` is a composer that grows with its text, sends on `Enter`, and takes a context chip and a `Stop reply` control. `@shipfox/react-ui/trace` is a collapsible tool row with running, succeeded, and failed states. `@shipfox/react-ui/suggestion` is a clickable prompt. All three are presentational.
