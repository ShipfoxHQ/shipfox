## Authentication

Shipfox connects to Notion through OAuth and acts as the installed Shipfox bot.
The person who authorizes Shipfox chooses which pages the bot can access.

Use a dedicated Notion service member to authorize Shipfox. Give that member
full access only to the pages and teamspaces that workflows need.

## Integration connection identity

The slug of a Notion integration connection defaults to `notion_<workspace>`,
where `<workspace>` is the normalized Notion workspace name. For example,
`notion_acme`. Shipfox makes the slug unique within the Shipfox workspace.

One Notion workspace can have one Shipfox integration connection. Connecting
the same Notion workspace again replaces the pages available to existing
workflows.

Use the slug as `source` for Notion events:

```yaml
triggers:
  on_notion_page:
    source: notion_acme
    event: page.properties_updated
```

For trigger field rules, see the [workflow schema reference](/reference/workflow-schema#trigger-fields).
