Connect Notion when a workflow needs Notion events or tools. See the [Notion
overview](/integrations/notion) for its capabilities and access boundary.

## Before you begin

Use a **dedicated Notion service member** to authorize Shipfox. This is the
recommended setup for every Notion workspace. Give the member full access only
to the pages and teamspaces that workflows need.

Shipfox acts as a bot, not as the person who authorizes it. The authorizing
member only decides which pages the bot can see. Only that member can share
more pages with the integration connection later.

A dedicated member keeps page sharing with one known account. It also avoids
depending on an employee account when that person leaves or loses access.

You need access to integration settings in the Shipfox workspace. You also
need access to the dedicated member's Notion account.

On Notion Enterprise, a workspace can restrict integrations to an approved
list. A Notion owner must approve **Shipfox** before the service member connects
it.

If the service member is unavailable, invite the Notion owner to Shipfox as a
member. The owner can then connect Notion from the Shipfox workspace.

## Authorize Notion

<Steps>
  <Step>

    **Start the integration connection**

    Open **Settings → Integrations** in the Shipfox workspace. Select
    **Notion**, then start the integration connection.
  </Step>
  <Step>

    **Sign in with the dedicated service member**

    Sign in to Notion with the service member. If Notion blocks the connection,
    ask a Notion owner to approve **Shipfox**.
  </Step>
  <Step>

    **Choose the pages Shipfox can access**

    Select the pages and teamspaces that workflows need. Child pages inherit
    access from a selected parent page.

    Connecting an already connected Notion workspace replaces the pages
    available to existing workflows.
  </Step>
  <Step>

    **Record the integration connection slug**

    Open the new integration connection's menu. Select **Use this integration**.
    Copy the slug from the event or tool example. Workflows use the slug as
    `source` or `connection`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To add pages later, the authorizing member shares them with **Shipfox** from
Notion's page menu. The workspace does not need to be connected again.

## Verify the integration connection

Open **Settings → Integrations**. Check that the Notion integration connection
is active and advertises Events and Agent tools.

In Notion, open one selected page and confirm that **Shipfox** appears in its
connections. Create a safe page or comment, then confirm that its event appears
under **Settings → Events** in Shipfox.
